## Operating notes that cost time to learn

- Lanes run under `codex exec --sandbox workspace-write`, which **denies git**.
  A lane cannot commit however firmly the spec asks; the architect commits the
  worktree. Do not write "commit on the current branch" into a lane spec.
- **Run the real checker; never reimplement it.** A hand re-derivation of the
  exec-bit inventory flagged 42 files where the actual test flagged one, because
  the hand version skipped the test's documented exclusions.
- **Run `bats tests/line-endings.bats` before every merge.** Three exec-bit
  regressions today; the third was on an already-tracked file, which a
  new-files-only check cannot see.
- A pipeline destroys the exit status a decision depends on
  (`cmd | filter || fallback` can never reach the fallback). Hit three times.
- Lane liveness is growth of the lane's log sampled twice with a window longer
  than the worker's think time — not `pgrep` against a worktree path.
