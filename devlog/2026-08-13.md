## 4. Standing rules that came out of today

- Mutate the production syscall, not a hook in front of it, when the claim is that the syscall ran.
- Assert the absence of the warning on the happy path. A permanently dead fsync must go red.
- Prefer a lock-holder and elapsed time over a larger sample when SQLite lock order is the subject.
- Do not record "the pragma does not wait" without measuring `busy_timeout` live and `busy_timeout=0`.
